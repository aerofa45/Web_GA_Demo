ALPINE.JS / PROGRESSIVE ENHANCEMENT

Before you publish.

A small editor guide enhanced with Alpine, separate from React’s DOM.

Publishing checklist

  • Use a descriptive page title and one primary heading.
  • Check link purpose, keyboard access, and image alternatives.
  • Confirm content dates with the department owner.
  • Preview at mobile and desktop widths before review.

What this demonstrates

Local Alpine state, disclosure semantics, and a readable no-JavaScript fallback. This guide does not submit a real CMS workflow.
